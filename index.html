Twenty-five generative geometry pieces, rendered live on-chain by the contract itself. Triangular Fibonacci shells — 148,200 points a piece — bloom from three seeded foci in one of four dealt palettes: Sunlight, Lotus, Neon, or Ultraviolet. Each piece folds through a slow four-minute cycle beneath a stippled construction lattice, its shells clustered in the outer field so it fills its frame edge to edge.
